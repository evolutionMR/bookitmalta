§ Frequently asked

The practical stuff.

Where do we set sail from?

Both La Zingara and Chardonnay sail from Sliema. You'll get the exact pontoon and crew contact in your booking confirmation once your date and boat are set.

How many guests can come aboard?

La Zingara takes up to 28 guests and Chardonnay up to 24. For larger parties we can hold both catamarans for the same day — mention it in your enquiry.

How does pricing work?

Every charter is private, so pricing varies with the boat, the season, and your group size and date. Send an enquiry and we'll reply with a transparent pricing range — no fixed seat price.

Can you arrange food, transfers, and decor?

Yes. A dedicated planner can sort onboard catering, transfers to Sliema, and decor for proposals, birthdays, or corporate days — just tell us what you have in mind.

Which boat should we choose?

La Zingara suits families, relaxed swim days, and proposals; Chardonnay suits bigger celebrations and corporate groups. Pick "Either" in the form and we'll recommend the best fit for your day.
