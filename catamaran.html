Good to know

The practical stuff.

Where exactly do we set sail from?

Both La Zingara and Chardonnay sail from Sliema. You’ll get the exact pontoon and the crew’s contact by email once your date and boat are confirmed.

Is the boat shared with anyone else?

No. Both catamarans are private charter only — the whole boat is yours for the day or the evening, and you never share the deck with another group.

Can we choose where we go?

Within reason, yes. Comino, the Blue Lagoon and Gozo are the usual full-day run; the sunset trip stays closer to the coast. The captain decides on the day what the sea allows.

How does pricing work?

Every charter is private, so pricing depends on the boat, the season, and your group size and date. Send an enquiry and we’ll reply with a transparent quote — no fixed seat price, and nothing to pay until your date is confirmed. Once you accept, a 10% deposit plus card processing holds the boat, and the balance goes to the operator on the day.

What about food and drink?

Bring your own — there’s a fridge, ice and coolers on board to keep everything cold. If you’d like anything arranged for a special occasion, mention it in your request and we’ll price it with your quote.

Which boat should we choose?

La Zingara is the party boat — birthdays, big celebrations and corporate groups up to 28. Chardonnay is the calmer of the two, better for families, relaxed swim days and proposals. Pick “Either” in the form and we’ll recommend the best fit for your day.

What happens if the weather is bad?

The captain decides on the morning. We move you to another date at no cost, and if no date works for you, the deposit is refunded less the card-processing cost.
