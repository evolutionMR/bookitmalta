Why direct

Less middleman, more boat.

When you book direct on BookItMalta, the operator running your charter is the one taking the booking — and most of your money. You get their actual name on the receipt, their phone number for last-minute questions, their crew waiting at the dock.

01

Pay the operator, not a middleman.

Global booking aggregators take 20-30% of every charter. BookItMalta takes a small fee that doesn't change the price you pay — operators keep more, prices stay sane.

02

Talk to the people running the boat.

Direct WhatsApp + email contact with the operator. Questions about your trip, dietary preferences, dock instructions — handled by the people who'll be on the water with you, not a global call centre.

03

Pay a deposit. Balance on the day.

Most operators take 15-25% deposit upfront, balance paid directly on the day. Lower commitment up front. Stripe Checkout handles the deposit securely — no card details ever touch BookItMalta.

04

Local Malta operators.

Every operator on the directory is Malta-based. They know the waters, the weather, the best coves on Comino. Not a generic "European charter platform" — actual local boats.
