§ 01 — The experience

A whole day on the water — no sharing, no schedule but yours

Spend the day exploring Comino's best spots — Santa Marija Bay, San Niklaw Bay, the Blue Lagoon and the Crystal Lagoon. Swim, snorkel, drift into sea caves, or just stretch out on deck. Full-day charters also head down to the south coast of Gozo.

It's a private boat — just your group. Your skipper sets the pace around what you feel like doing, with nowhere else to be.
