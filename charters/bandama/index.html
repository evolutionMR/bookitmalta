The experience

Malta's coastline, in complete privacy.

Experience Malta's coastline in complete privacy aboard Bandama, your exclusive luxury day charter.

Cruise through the breathtaking waters of Comino and Gozo, discovering hidden coves, tranquil bays, and crystal-clear lagoons away from the crowds. Spend the day swimming, snorkelling, sunbathing, and relaxing while enjoying a bespoke charter designed entirely around you.

From the iconic Blue Lagoon to secret anchorages known only to locals, every journey aboard Bandama is crafted to deliver elegance, comfort, and unforgettable memories.

Departure 09:30 or 10:00 from Ta' Xbiex Creek · Return 17:30 or 18:00 · 30 NM cruising range covers Comino, Blue Lagoon, and southern Gozo · Route flexes with weather and your group's preferences.
