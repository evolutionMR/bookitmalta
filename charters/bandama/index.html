Where you cast off

A private boat charter, moments from Sliema.

Bandama berths at Ta' Xbiex Creek, a sheltered marina on the same harbour as Sliema — many people simply call it the Sliema marina. It's a ten-minute walk along the waterfront from the Sliema ferries, and a short taxi or drive from the hotels along The Strand.

That matters more than it sounds. Most private charters on the big marketplaces stay vague about exactly where you meet the boat — you book first and chase the pin later. Here you know from the outset: you board on a calm, sheltered creek in the heart of the harbour, not a far northern jetty, so the cruising day starts the moment you step aboard.

From the berth it's open water within minutes, then a straight run to Comino, the Blue Lagoon and the southern coast of Gozo — the same route whether you're a family who'd rather not sail, a group marking an occasion, or first-timers who want a steady motor yacht underfoot rather than a heeling sail.

Good to know

Departing beside Sliema, in plain English.

Where does the Bandama charter depart from in Malta?

Bandama departs from Ta' Xbiex Creek Marina — the central harbour marina many people know as the Sliema marina, a ten-minute walk along the waterfront from the Sliema ferries. Your exact berth and meeting time are confirmed the moment the captain accepts your date.

Is this a private charter or a shared boat trip?

Entirely private. The boat, the skipper and the route are yours for the day — up to eight guests, no strangers aboard. You book directly with the operator, never a seat on a shared-tour boat.

What does the day on the water cover?

A full day — roughly 09:30 to 17:30 — cruising Comino, the Blue Lagoon and the southern coast of Gozo with a professional skipper and crew. Fuel for up to 30 nautical miles, mineral water, soft drinks and snacks are included. €1,850 is the total for the whole boat, not a per-person price.
