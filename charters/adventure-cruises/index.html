The operator

Sliema-based, powerboat-first.

Adventure Cruises Sliema Ltd. operates powerboat tours from Sliema Ferries seven days a week, weather permitting. Three distinct tour formats — a private full-day charter that's all yours, a day trip with the signature Comino swim stops, and a night harbour cruise that hits Valletta and the Three Cities from the water.

The operator handles the boat, the route, the timing, and the on-water experience. Routes flex with weather and sea conditions on the day — you'll be briefed on departure.

What you can expect

  • Daily departures from Sliema Ferries
  • Private and shared booking options
  • Free snorkel masks on Blue Lagoon trips
  • Swim stops at Crystal Lagoon, Blue Lagoon, halfa rock Gozo
  • Night harbour route covering Valletta + Three Cities
  • Direct WhatsApp + phone contact with the operator

Pricing varies by tour type, party size, and season. Confirmed on enquiry.

Adventure Cruises confirms availability within 24 hours via email and phone, sends you a deposit payment link, and confirms your booking the moment the deposit clears. Direct card capture inside the booking flow is launching soon — for now, deposits are handled via the operator's secure payment link (Revolut, PayPal, or bank transfer).

Booking direct

Why this isn't a marketplace.

You're booking directly with Adventure Cruises — Malta-based operator running their own tours, on their own terms.

  • Receipt comes from Adventure Cruises Sliema Ltd. — not from BookItMalta
  • Phone + email goes directly to the operator running your tour
  • Deposit at booking; balance settled directly with the operator
  • Cancellation policy is the operator's, not a global aggregator's
  • BookItMalta handles the booking infrastructure; the operator handles the boat
