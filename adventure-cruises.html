§ Frequently asked

The practical stuff.

Where do we meet?

Sliema Ferries — look for the Adventure Cruises boats (white powerboats with our name on the hull) and crew at the dock. You'll get the exact pontoon and crew contact in your confirmation message. Please arrive 15 minutes before departure so we can board on time.

What happens in bad weather?

If the captain calls it (high winds, rough sea), we cancel and process a full refund of the booking fee within 24 hours. You'll get email + WhatsApp notification the morning of the tour.

Can I bring my own food & drink?

Yes — fully encouraged. The boat has coolers for your drinks — bring your own water and ice. Most guests bring a picnic; the Blue Lagoon stop also has beach kiosks if you forget.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Life vests in every size are on board, including child sizes. Discounted child rates apply — message us via WhatsApp with the ages of the kids in your group and we'll send the exact pricing for the day.

How do I pay the €30/seat balance on the day?

The €30-per-seat balance is settled in cash with the crew when you board. Tips are optional but always appreciated — €5–10 per guest is normal for a good day.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before departure for a full refund of the booking fee. Inside 24 hours, the fee is non-refundable but we'll happily move you to another day if seats are available.
