§ Frequently asked

The practical stuff.

Where do we meet?

Sliema Ferries dock — the wooden pontoon at the south end. Look for the Adventure Cruises sign. Please arrive 15 minutes before departure so we can board on time. ?? confirm exact pontoon

What happens in bad weather?

If the captain calls it (high winds, rough sea), we cancel and process a full refund of the booking fee within 24 hours. You'll get email + WhatsApp notification the morning of the tour.

Can I bring my own food & drink?

Yes — fully encouraged. The boat has fridges, ice, and coolers. Most guests bring a picnic; the Blue Lagoon stop also has beach kiosks if you forget.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Life vests in every size are on board. Under-3s are free; ages 3–12 are €25/seat (half price); 13+ pay the full €40. ?? confirm child pricing — your call

How do I pay the €30/seat balance on the day?

The crew accepts cash, card (POS terminal on board), Revolut, and Apple/Google Pay. Most guests pay cash because it's fastest at boarding. Tips are optional but always appreciated — €5–10 per guest is normal for a good day.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before departure for a full refund of the booking fee. Inside 24 hours, the fee is non-refundable but we'll happily move you to another day if seats are available.
